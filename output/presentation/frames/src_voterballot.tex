

\begin{frame}{Litigation Pushes Voters Off the Mayoral Ballot}
\hypertarget{src:voterballot}{}
\small
Turnout is compulsory in Brazil for ages 18--69 and so is institutionally floored, but a voter can still decline to choose, casting a blank or null ballot that elects no one. With turnout pinned, demobilization appears as a rise in these non-valid votes and a fall in valid ones. That is the mayoral pattern below; the pooled council ballot stays flat.
