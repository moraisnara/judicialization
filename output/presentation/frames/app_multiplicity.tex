
\begin{frame}{Appendix --- Many Outcomes, No Family Cut}
\hypertarget{app:multiplicity}{}
\footnotesize
The consolidation rests on a family of correlated outcomes. Two routes test whether it survives multiple-testing correction without declaring which outcomes ``count.''
\vspace{-2pt}

\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.52\linewidth}
\scriptsize \textbf{(a) Collapse each theory-declared family to one index} (inverse-covariance weighted \ns{\citep{anderson2008}} and equal-weight \ns{\citep{kling2007}} indices). One family $=$ one test. Each 2024 index is net of its own 2016 baseline; effects in SD units, positive $=$ consolidation.
\vspace{-4pt}
\begin{center}
\scriptsize
\scalebox{0.95}{\resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{\input{../tables/tex/summary_indices.tex}}}
\end{center}
\scriptsize The overall combined index clears ($p=\IdxOverallP$) and closeness clears on its own ($p=\IdxClosenessP$); concentration does not ($p=\IdxConcentrationP$), and representation and the council-ballot placebo are null. \IdxNonBallotK{} of the \IdxOverallK{} outcomes in the overall index are not ballot outcomes, and the ballot family alone is $p=\IdxWithdrawalP$, so the index establishes the consolidation rather than a face.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.46\linewidth}
\scriptsize \textbf{(b) Romano--Wolf stepdown} \ns{\citep{romano2005,romano2016}} over all \RWFamilyN{} barrier/representation reduced forms, a \emph{joint} wild-cluster bootstrap ($B=9{,}999$, shared Rademacher weights) controlling the family-wise error rate.
\vspace{-4pt}
\begin{center}
\scriptsize
\scalebox{0.95}{\resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{\input{../tables/tex/romano_wolf_stepdown.tex}}}
\end{center}
\scriptsize On reduced-form $p$-values, margin and runner-up survive Holm ($p=\RFHolmMarginP$, $\RFHolmRunnerUpP$) \ns{\citep{holm1979}}. Romano--Wolf, which carries the \ARNClusters{}-cluster tails through the bootstrap, clears them only at 10\% ($p=\RWMarginP$, $\RWRunnerUpP$). On 2SLS $p$-values none of the \MultFamilyN{} survives Holm or BH \ns{\citep{benjamini1995}}; margin Holm $p=\MultMarginHolm$.
\end{column}
\end{columns}
