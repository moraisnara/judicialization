\section{The template: Ash, Morelli \& Vannoni}

The design follows the shift-share instrumental-variables architecture of Ash, Morelli
\& Vannoni, \emph{More Laws, More Growth? Evidence from U.S.\ States}. Their data are at the
state ($s$) $\times$ biennium ($t$) level, $W_{st}$ the number of legal provisions
enacted, $\Delta \log Y_{st}$ the log change in real per-capita GDP. The structural
equation --- hence the second stage --- is
%
\begin{equation}
\Delta \log Y_{st} \;=\; \alpha_s + \alpha_t + \alpha_s \cdot t
  \;+\; \rho \log W_{st} \;+\; X_{st}'\beta \;+\; \varepsilon_{st},
\end{equation}
%
where $\alpha_s$ are state fixed effects, $\alpha_t$ biennium fixed effects, and
$\alpha_s \cdot t$ state-specific linear trends. The instrument for $\log W_{st}$ is the
topic-share-weighted sum of leave-one-out national legislative flows,
%
\begin{equation}
Z_{st} \;=\; \sum_{k=1}^{K}
  \underbrace{\frac{W^{k}_{s0}}{W_{s0}}}_{\text{shares}} \;\cdot\;
  \underbrace{\frac{1}{49}\sum_{r \neq s} \Delta \log W^{k}_{rt}}_{\text{shifts}} ,
\end{equation}
%
where shares are pre-treatment topic shares averaged over 1955--1964 (period $0$), and
shifts are the leave-one-out \emph{average} log change in legislation on topic $k$ across
the other 49 states. $Z_{st}$ is standardized to mean zero, variance one. First stage and
reduced form:
%
\begin{align}
\log W_{st} &\;=\; \alpha_s + \alpha_t + \alpha_s \cdot t
  \;+\; \psi Z_{st} \;+\; X_{st}'\beta \;+\; \eta_{st}, \\[-1pt]
\Delta \log Y_{st} &\;=\; \alpha_s + \alpha_t + \alpha_s \cdot t
  \;+\; \gamma Z_{st} \;+\; X_{st}'\beta \;+\; \varepsilon_{st}.
\end{align}

\section{What this paper estimates}

The unit is the municipality $m$, the window the 2020 $\to$ 2024 municipal cycle, the
race mayoral. With $Z_m$ the shift-share instrument, $\Delta W_m$ the change in
judicialization, $X_m$ predetermined controls and $\alpha_{uf(m)}$ a state (UF) fixed
effect, the \textbf{first stage} is
%
\begin{equation}
\Delta W_m \;=\; \psi Z_m \;+\; X_m'\gamma \;+\; \alpha_{uf(m)} \;+\; \eta_m ,
\end{equation}
%
and the \textbf{second stage}, written in the AMV form of (1), takes the outcome in
differences:
%
\begin{equation}
\Delta Y_{m} \;=\; \beta\, \Delta W_m \;+\; X_m'\gamma \;+\; \alpha_{uf(m)}
  \;+\; \varepsilon_m ,
\end{equation}
%
with $\Delta W_m$ instrumented by $Z_m$. This is the analogue of AMV's $\Delta \log
Y_{st}$; our outcomes are vote shares and rates, so they enter in levels rather than
logs. 


\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Which baseline?} $\Delta Y_m = Y_{m,2024} - Y_{m,b}$ admits two
readings. With $b = 2020$ the difference is \emph{treatment-window aligned}: the outcome
moves over exactly the window in which $\Delta W_m$ is measured. But 2020 is also the
year the exposure shares are drawn from, and it is a covid cycle, so the base is not
clean. With $b = 2016$ the baseline is a \emph{clean pre-window}: 2016 predates the 2020
shares entirely and cannot be contaminated by the instrument, at the cost of an outcome
window (2016--2024) wider than the treatment window.


\smallskip
\noindent $X_m$ is a block of predetermined, largely \textbf{Census-based} controls (see
\S3). Standard errors are clustered at the \textbf{state} level ($G = 26$): the
leave-own-state-out shift is constant within a state, and first-instance litigation is
correlated within state through the TRE. 
\section{Variable construction}

\subsection*{Instrument $Z_m$ }

\begin{equation*}
Z_m \;=\; \sum_{k} s_{m,k,2020} \cdot g_k^{(-uf)} ,
\qquad
g_k^{(-uf)} = \log\!\big(1 + N^{-uf}_{k,2024}\big) - \log\!\big(1 + N^{-uf}_{k,2020}\big) .
\end{equation*}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Shares} $s_{m,k,2020}$: municipality $m$'s 2020 count of lawsuits on
subject $k$, divided by \emph{its own} total 2020 adversarial caseload. Shares sum to one
within municipality, so $Z_m$ is a weighted \emph{average} of shocks, not a scaled
exposure level.
\item \textbf{Shifts} $g_k^{(-uf)}$: log growth of the leave-own-state-out aggregate
count of filings on subject $k$, pooled over the other 25 states. National and own-UF
grids are subtracted wide, so a (UF, subject, year) cell with no own cases receives
$\text{national} - 0$, never $0$.
\item \textbf{Topic level} $k$: the leaf TSE subject code \code{CD\_ASSUNTO\_PRINCIPAL},
no roll-up. \textbf{Universe}: first-instance cases only, filed \emph{before} the
first-round election day (15 Nov 2020; 6 Oct 2024), municipality of origin resolved from
the SIG export.
\item \textbf{Mandatory-filing exclusion}: administrative and procedural classes and
subjects --- candidate registration (RRC), party lists (DRAP), campaign-finance accounts,
enforcement codes: roughly 48.5\% of all filings --- are dropped first. What remains is
the \emph{adversarial} universe.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Endogenous variable $\Delta W_m$}

\begin{equation*}
\Delta W_m \;=\; \log\!\big(1 + L_{m,2024}\big) - \log\!\big(1 + L_{m,2020}\big) ,
\end{equation*}

\noindent where $L_{m,t}$ counts adversarial, first-instance, pre-election lawsuits
originating in municipality $m$ in cycle $t$, summed over all retained subjects --- the
quantity labelled ``Judicialization'' in every table.


\subsection*{Controls $X_m$}

All predetermined, and the core block is \textbf{2010 Census structure} --- population,
urbanisation, income per capita, schooling.
